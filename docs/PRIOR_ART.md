# Prior Art: CtPU and CCtPU Terminology

## Concept Definition
- **CtPU** (Conversion to Physical Units): Linear conversion of ADC counts to SI units (k·x + b) for software-defined instrumentation.
- **CCtPU** (Calibrated Conversion to Physical Units): Two-point calibration (Zero/Span) against physical reference standards.

## Independent Origin
These terms and their specific application to software-defined metrology for FX2-based oscilloscopes were independently conceptualized and implemented by dtba3a-del in August 2026.

## Disclaimer & Distinction
CtPU/CCtPU in this context describes independent research in software-defined instrumentation and metrological conversion for ADC data. It is conceptually and practically distinct from, and not affiliated with:
- Tensor Processing Units (machine learning accelerators)
- Current Transformer Protection Units (electrical protection hardware)
- Crank Trigger Pick-Up sensors (automotive)

Any similarity to existing acronyms in unrelated technical domains is coincidental. The conceptual framework, implementation, and application domain here are independently developed.
